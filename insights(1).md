# Germany 2022 FIFA World Cup Failure Investigation

## Root Cause Analysis -- Final Insights

## Project Summary

This project investigates the reasons behind Germany's group-stage
elimination from the FIFA World Cup 2022 using a complete data analytics
workflow. Match statistics were collected from multiple football data
sources, organized into a PostgreSQL database, analyzed using SQL,
validated with match footage, and prepared for visualization in Power
BI.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Attack Analysis

## Problem 1 -- Did Germany create enough attacking opportunities?

**Finding**

Germany consistently created more attacking opportunities than their
opponents through effective build-up play, final-third entries, crosses,
and big chances.

**Insight**

Germany's inability to create chances was **not** a major reason for
elimination.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Problem 2 -- Was poor finishing one of the main reasons for Germany's elimination?

**Finding**

Germany generated numerous high-quality chances but converted too few
into goals, particularly against Japan and Spain. Several missed
opportunities could have changed the outcome of those matches.

**Insight**

Poor finishing was the **primary reason** behind Germany's World Cup
exit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Problem 3 -- Did Germany struggle to provide quality service to the attackers?

**Finding**

Germany's midfield and wide players consistently supplied quality
service through progressive passing, final-third combinations, and
accurate crosses.

**Insight**

The attackers received sufficient support. The problem was not chance
creation but converting those chances.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Problem 4 -- Were Germany's attackers effective in threatening the opposition goal?

**Finding**

Germany consistently threatened the opposition goal through touches
inside the box and frequent big-chance creation.

**Insight**

Germany's attackers generated enough danger but lacked the finishing
efficiency required to win matches.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Midfield Analysis

## Problem 5 -- Did Germany control the midfield effectively?

**Finding**

Germany controlled possession, maintained a high passing accuracy, and
dictated the tempo in most matches.

**Insight**

Midfield control was not a weakness throughout the tournament.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Problem 6 -- Did Germany progress the ball efficiently into dangerous attacking areas?

**Finding**

Germany consistently progressed the ball into dangerous attacking areas
using effective build-up play and final-third passing.

**Insight**

Ball progression was not a contributing factor to Germany's elimination.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Problem 7 -- Did the midfield create enough attacking opportunities?

**Finding**

Germany's midfield consistently created attacking opportunities through
progressive passing, through balls, and final-third creativity.

**Insight**

Midfield creativity successfully supported the attacking unit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Problem 8 -- Did Germany recover possession effectively after losing the ball?

**Finding**

Germany recorded recoveries and interceptions that were generally
comparable to their opponents. However, they repeatedly lost important
ground and aerial duels during transition moments, allowing opponents to
continue dangerous attacks. Spain managed these situations particularly
well.

**Insight**

Overall recovery and interception numbers were competitive, but failure
to win key physical duels during defensive transitions became an
important factor behind several goals conceded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Defensive Analysis

## Problem 9 -- Was Germany's defensive performance strong enough throughout the tournament?

**Finding**

Germany struggled to consistently win decisive duels, clear dangerous
situations, and prevent opponents from capitalizing on defensive
mistakes.

**Insight**

Germany's defensive structure lacked consistency during crucial moments,
contributing directly to goals conceded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## -- Were Germany's defensive actions effective?

**Finding**

Although interceptions and recoveries were reasonable, Germany failed to
consistently win decisive defensive actions.

**Insight**

Germany's defensive actions were inconsistent when they mattered most.

------------------------------------------------------------------------


## Problem 10 -- Did Germany compete effectively in physical battles?

**Finding**

Germany's success rate in ground and aerial duels was generally lower
than their opponents during crucial phases of matches.

**Insight**

Failure to consistently win physical battles reduced Germany's ability
to stop attacks, protect leads, and regain control of matches.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Goalkeeping Analysis

## Problem 11 -- Did Germany receive the required level of goalkeeping performance?

**Finding**

Germany's goalkeeper made routine saves but failed to produce decisive
interventions during crucial moments. Several rebounds and unsuccessful
save attempts contributed to opposition goals.

**Insight**

Goalkeeping performance was below the level required to positively
influence match outcomes and became one of the primary reasons for
Germany's elimination.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Problem 12 -- Did the goalkeeper effectively manage defensive situations?

**Finding**

High claims and punches were infrequent for both Germany and their
opponents.

**Insight**

The available data does not provide strong evidence that penalty-area
management was a significant weakness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Set Piece Analysis

## Problem -- Did Germany make effective use of set-piece opportunities?

**Finding**

Germany created opportunities from set pieces but failed to convert them
consistently. One goal was also ruled out for offside.

**Insight**

Set-piece efficiency remained below expectations and provided limited
attacking impact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Root Cause Analysis

## Primary Causes

### 1. Poor Finishing

-   Germany consistently created high-quality attacking opportunities.
-   Big chance conversion remained low.
-   Missed opportunities against Japan and Spain proved decisive.

### 2. Defensive Weaknesses

-   Failure to win crucial ground and aerial duels.
-   Defensive mistakes and poor clearances resulted in goals conceded.
-   Defensive inconsistency during key moments significantly affected
    match outcomes.

### 3. Goalkeeping Performance

-   Routine saves were made.
-   Decisive saves were lacking during important moments.
-   Rebounds and unsuccessful save attempts contributed to opposition
    goals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Secondary Contributing Factor

### Defensive Transition

Germany's recoveries and interceptions were generally competitive.
However, stronger anticipation and additional
interceptions---particularly from midfield during defensive
transitions---could have prevented several dangerous attacks from
developing into goal-scoring situations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Factors That Were Not Major Causes

-   Attacking opportunity creation
-   Midfield control
-   Ball progression
-   Midfield creativity

These aspects consistently performed well according to the statistical
analysis.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Conclusion

Germany's elimination was **not** caused by an inability to create
chances or control matches. The team's midfield successfully controlled
possession, progressed the ball, and supplied quality opportunities to
the forwards.

The primary causes of Germany's group-stage exit were poor finishing,
defensive weaknesses---particularly the inability to win crucial duels
and defend key moments---and below-par goalkeeping during decisive
situations.

Overall, Germany produced enough attacking football to progress beyond
the group stage, but repeated failures in converting chances and
defending critical moments ultimately led to their early elimination.
